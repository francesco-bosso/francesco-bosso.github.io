About Me

# Who am I?

Who am I?

I’m Francesco Bosso, an AI Research Engineer passionate about building intelligent systems that make sense of complex data and support real-world applications. I collaborate with open research communities and contribute to ESA’s PyRaws library, among other initiatives. I also serve as an expert evaluator for research proposals within the Horizon Europe framework.

What do I do?

My work lies at the intersection of machine learning, geospatial AI, sensing, defence, and space, where I focus on turning research ideas into technologies that extract actionable insights and deliver practical impact. Over the years, I’ve contributed to projects spanning next-generation aerospace systems, including the Global Combat Air Programme (GCAP) for the development of the sixth-generation fighter aircraft, as well as AI for environmental forecasting and security policy. At the European Commission’s Joint Research Centre, I applied Large Language Models and geospatial AI to extract knowledge from unstructured data and support evidence-based decision-making on topics such as hybrid threats. My patent and research focus on cognitive sensing, reinforcement learning, and sensor fusion for integrating multiple data sources into coherent, actionable information. Ultimately, I’m driven by a simple idea: to use AI to solve meaningful and technically challenging problems in a way that is both rigorous and useful.
